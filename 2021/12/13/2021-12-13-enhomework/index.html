英语作业

发表于 2021-12-13
你懂

Nowadays, it’s quite common for us students to have our own mobile phones, but we are not allowed to bring them to school. In my opinion, the rule is good for both our health and studies.

Last year, we didn’t go to school for about 2 months because of the pandemic. One day, while I was taking class on the Internet, one of my classmates called me and asked me to play video games on the phone and I agreed. From then on, I began to play games, chat with my classmates and watch short videos instead of listening to the teacher. I found it fun to play on the phone because it seemed to open a wonderful world in which there were so many interesting things, so many exciting games, so many funny videos— Compared with them, lessons and homework in the realistic world were boring and hard. Soon I was so addicted to playing on the phone that I spent almost all my time on the phone and often stayed up late swiping the phone. As a result, my eyesight was badly affected. What was even worse was that I failed the test, which made me really upset. Looking at the awful score on my test paper, I thought for a long time and finally realized that it was because I played with the phone that I didn’t do well in the test. So I decided to keep away from the mobile phone and forced myself to focus on my schoolwork. Luckily, my grades improved through my efforts.

I believe many other students also had, have or will have such an experience in using the phone because we are too young to control and manage ourselves. To avoid being affected by the mobile phone, there is no doubt that the rule that students aren’t allowed to bring phones to school plays a positive role in making sure the students’ development. Only in this way, can we use the mobile phone properly.
